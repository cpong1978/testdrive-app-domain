The conditions for using this personally operated desktop setup.

Effective date: 5 November 2026

1. Purpose and availability

testDrive is a personal Windows desktop setup operated by cpong1978 that uses Rclone to access an authorized Google Drive account. This website provides information and policies; it does not offer public account registration, file storage or a multi-user sharing service.

2. Authorized use

Only connect accounts and access files that you own or are authorized to use. Do not use the setup to bypass another person's permissions, distribute harmful content or interfere with Google, GitHub or another user's services. Access remains subject to the permissions granted by the Google account owner and applicable provider terms.

3. File changes and local caching

Applications using the mounted drive can make changes under the authorized account. Confirm the intended account and destination before modifying or deleting files. A local write may finish before its background upload to Google Drive completes; verify upload completion before shutting down or clearing pending data.

Maintain independent backups for important files. This setup is not a guarantee against accidental deletion, conflicting edits, service interruptions or data loss.

4. Credentials and access

The Google account owner authorizes access through Google OAuth and can revoke it through Google Account settings. Keep configuration files and OAuth tokens private. Revoking remote access does not remove copies already downloaded to a computer.

5. Service dependencies

Access depends on the operator's computer, internet connection, available storage, valid authorization and Google's services and limits. No continuous availability, fixed transfer speed, recovery time or real-time synchronization is promised.

6. Privacy and provider terms

The Privacy policy explains the setup's use of Google data and local storage. Google Drive, Google APIs, Rclone and GitHub remain subject to their own applicable terms and licenses. testDrive is not operated or endorsed by Google.

7. Changes and contact

Changes to these terms will be reflected in the effective date. Contact the operator at cpong1978@gmail.com with questions or to report an access issue.
